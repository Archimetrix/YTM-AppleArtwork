# Changelog

## 1.2.1
- First public release.
- Plays Apple Music animated (HLS) album artwork over the YouTube Music player thumbnail, fetched directly from Apple.
- Settings entry inside YouTube Music: on/off switch, persistent log (copy / share / clear), clear artwork cache.
- Works with root installs (no manifest changes).
- Automatic recovery when Android rejects a video (retry, then next variant).
- Package renamed to `dev.archimetrix` (own namespace).
